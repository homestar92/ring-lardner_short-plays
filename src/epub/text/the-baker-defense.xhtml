The Baker Defense

Scene—Home of Mr. and Mrs. Brown in Evanston.

Time—Sunday morning in January.

Mrs. B. shivering It's terribly cold in the house! She looks at the thermometer. Why, it's down to 40! Do you suppose the fire can be out?
Mr. B. It might be.
Mrs. B. It must be! And that's the second time this week he's let it go out. Why don't we get a new furnace man?
Mr. B. I'm satisfied with the one we have.
Mrs. B. But we'll all freeze.
Mr. B. smiling Oh, no. You don't freeze at 40.
Mrs. B. But I'm nearly frozen now.
Mr. B. I think I can guarantee that the house will be comfortable by July.
Mrs. B. But what's the sense of keeping a man who lets the fire go out all the time?
Mr. B. He doesn't let it go out all the time. He lets it go out a very small part of the time, and the rest of the time it stays out.
Mrs. B. I do wish you'd go down and fix it.
Mr. B. There's no coal.
Mrs. B. No coal! Why didn't you order some?
Mr. B. Why, we just ran out this morning, and I can't order any on Sunday. They're closed up.
Mrs. B. The children will all be sick again!
Mr. B. They might be.
Mrs. B. And there'll be more doctor bills. By the way, did you ever pay Dr. Flynn for last summer?
Mr. B. Why, no. He lives away out in Englewood.
Mrs. B. I can't stand it if all four of them get sick again.
Mr. B. You should worry. I saw Sam Taylor yesterday, and he said all five of his were sick. We're doing better than that.
Mrs. B. Heavens! It's a quarter of eleven. We'll be late to church.
Mr. B. Well, the Greens were late last Sunday.
Mrs. B. And we'll never be able to walk there in this snow. Did you order a carriage?
Mr. B. No. I thought perhaps one would stop fo rus by mistake.
Mrs. B. Maybe there won't be any service this kind of a day. I gues we'd better call up.
Mr. B. Oh, no. If we get there and find the doors locked, we'll know it's off.
Mrs. B. Have you any money for the collection?
Mr. B. I don't know. If i have it's in my pocket.
Mrs. B. Why don't you look?
Mr. B. I did look—Thursday night.
Mrs. B. Did you have any then?
Mr. B. Yes. Half a dollar.
Mrs. B. Did you spend any of it?
Mr. B. I don't remember.
Mrs. B. Why, you haven't shaved!
Mr. B. Yes, I have. Lots of times.
Mrs. B. When did you shave last?
Mr. B. Wednesday, I believe.
Mrs. B. You can't go to church looking like that.
Mr. B. I'm satisfied with the way I look.
Olga enters, excited.
Olga A water pipe's busted! In the basement. You'd better call a plumber.
Mr. B. I may meet one on the street tomorrow.
Olga But this whole floor will be flooded by dinner time.
Mr. B. We can eat upstairs.
