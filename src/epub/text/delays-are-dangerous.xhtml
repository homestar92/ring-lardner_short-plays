Delays Are Dangerous

Characters.

  • William Gleason, a Trusted Lieutenant.

  • Harry Lord, a Noble Captain.

  • Ralph Plague, a Chicago Bug.

  • George Dime, a Bellhop.

Scene I

The lobby of a California hotel. It is evening. Gleason is seated in a comfortable chair digesting his supper. Enter Lord.

Lord All organized, Kid. But there's only four of us, not counting you.
Gleason Who've ya got?
Lord Scotty and Ping and Joe and myself.
Gleason Good bunch outside o' Ping. Go ahead, Harry, and I'll be up in a couple o' minutes. Got plenty o' cards?
Lord We'll get some new ones. Hurry it along. Room 324.
Gleason All right, I'll be right there. I want to buy a couple o' stamps.
Exit Lord. Gleason walks toward the cigar counter. Dime accosts him.
Dime Mr. Gleason? Gentleman here to see you.
Gleason What's his name?
Dime Mr. Plague from Chicago.
Gleason I don't know no Plague.
Dime There he stands, by that pillar.
Mr. Plague advances and Dime exits.
Plague Mr. Gleason?
Gleason Yeh. What can I do for you?
Plague My name is Plague, Mr. Gleason. I guess you don't remember me.
Gleason I guess not.
Plague I'm from old Chi. I met you oncet out to the ball park. O'Neill introduced me to ya.
Gleason O, yeh.
Plague I thought I'd look ya up.
Gleason Yeh, glad to see ya.
Enter Dime.
Dime Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says they're waitin' for ya.
Gleason Tell him I'll be right up.
Exit Dime.
Plague I won't keep ya long.
Gleason That's all right.
Plague Well, what about Walsh?
Gleason He's all right.
Plague Think he'll be as good as ever?
Gleason Sure.
Enter Dime.
Dime Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says they're waitin' for ya.
Gleason I'll be right along.
Exit Dime.
Plague What d'ya think o' the Federal league?
Gleason Pretty hard to tell yet.
Plague They're spendin' a lot o' dough.
Gleason Yeh.
Plague How's it come you didn't go with 'em?
Gleason Me? They don't want me. They're lookin' for stars.
Plague I heard they was after ya.
Gleason Nothin' to it.
Plague Any o' the young fellahs look good?
Gleason Sure.
Plague What about Roth?
Gleason Looks pretty good.
Enter Dime.
Dime Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says "Hurry up."
Gleason Comin' right up.
Exit Dime.
Plague Mebbe you want to go somewheres.
Gleason That's all right.
Plague How's Comiskey?
Gleason He's all right, I guess.
Plague I heard he was pretty sick.
Gleason Nothin' to it.
Plague Goin' to hold onto Bodie?
Gleason Sure.
Plague That Chappell turned out to be a kind o' lemon didn't he?
Gleason He's a good ball player.
Plague He didn't hig very good when he broke in.
Gleason Nobody hits all the time.
Enter Dime.
Dime Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord wants to know if you're comin'.
Gleason Tell him I don't know. No, tell him I'll be along pretty quick.
Gleason winks at Dime as latter exits.
Plague Am I keepin' ya?
Gleason That's all right.
Plague Where's Callahan at?
Gleason Went to church, I guess.
Plague Do ball players like him?
Gleason Sure. Why not?
Plague Oh, nothin'. Who's goin' to win the pennant?
Gleason What pennant?
Plague 'Merican league.
Gleason Well, it's between eight teams.
Plague You fellahs got any chancet?
Gleason No, we've give up. We ain't even goin' to try.
Plague I don't think the Athaletics can come back.
Gleason Where from?
Plague I mean, I don't think they can repeat.
Enter Dime.
Dime Call for Mr. Plague.
Plague Who wants me?
Gleason Damfi know.
Plague Well, see you later.
Exit Plague.
Gleason Yes, you will.
Dime Better duck while the goin's good.
Gleason Here's somethin for your trouble.
Dime Thanks.
Dime exits. Gleason starts toward elevator, but meets Lord.
Gleason What's the matter?
Lord I couldn't keep 'em any longer. They wouldn't stand for a four-handed game.
Gleason Where are they at?
Lord Scotty and Joe went to a show and Ping went after a sandwich, I guess. What was the matte with you?
Gleason That ⸻! If I was drinkin' I'd take some absinthe.
Lord Who's this guy coming?
Exit Gleason.
