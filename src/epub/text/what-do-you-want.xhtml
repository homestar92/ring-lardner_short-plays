What Do You Want?

Play in One Act

Scene—The Office. Time— Thursday Afternoon.

The editor is sitting at his desk. There enters a middle aged gentleman of apparently sound body.
Gent Sporting Editor?
Ed. No. He won't be in till tonight.
Gent Could you tell me where I might find Sport Donnelly? He's an old football player.
Ed. Yes, I've heard of him. If I were you I'd call up Ed Smith at Main 1050.
Gent Would you mind calling him up for me? I'm not very good at talking over the phone.
The editor complies and returns to his desk.
Ed. He stops over at the C. A. A.
Gent Thanks. He starts out, hesitates, and returns to the desk. Would you mind calling up and finding out whether he is there?
The editor complies and returns to his desk
Ed. They say you'd probably find him at the I. A. C.
Gent Thanks. He starts out , hesitates, and returns to the desk. Would you mind—
The editor not only starts out but goes through with it.
