Gloom

Characters

  • Kid Gleason, Assistant Manager.

  • Buck Weaver, a Captain.

Scene—A Hotel in Washington.

Gleason Well, Buck, you're captain now.
Weaver Yes.
Gleason What are you goin' to do tonight?
Weaver I don't know.
Gleason What shall we do?
Weaver Howinell do I know?
Gleason Want to play a little cards?
Weaver Sure, I'll play a little.
Gleason Who shall we get?
Weaver I don't know. They've all gone out but Ping and I and you and Joe and Lena.
Gleason Well, that's enough to start a game.
Weaver Well, go ahead and start it.
Gleason No, you go ahead and start it.
Weaver No, you.
Gleason No, you.
Weaver There, Ping and Joe's went out.
Gleason There goes Lena, too.
Weaver Well, what shall we do?
Gleason Let's go to a nickel show.
Weaver All right.
Gleason If he'd of stuck, they'd of been a game organized before supper.
Weaver Sure, but they ain't nobody else that has sense enough to get the bunch together.
Gleason Well, let's go to a nickel show.
Weaver All right. I'm with you.
They start out.
Gleason And five months more to go.
Weaver 'Ell.
