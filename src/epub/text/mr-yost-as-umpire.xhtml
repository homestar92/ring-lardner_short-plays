Mr. Yost as Umpire

Scene—Staggs field. Time—Thanksgiving afternoon.

Indignant Player to Referee "Say, this guy held me."
Referee to Umpire "Did somebody hold this guy?"
Umpire to Indignant Player "Let me tell you somethin'. When you saw this quarterback start towards cheer, you ought to have been right cheer. Instead of that, you were where? You were there when you ought to have been cheer. When you saw this man start over there, you ought to have run over cheer, instead of standing still cheer. He was going to do what? He was going to pass. But you stood still cheer when you ought to have run over there."
Indignant Player "But he held me."
Umpire "Let me tll you somethin'. He's a good ball player."
