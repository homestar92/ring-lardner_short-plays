Small Talk at Breakfast (II)

Characters

  • John.

  • Jim.

  • We.

John What color is my pajamas?
We White and blue.
John What color is my p'liceman's suit?
We Blue.
Jim What color's mine?
John You haven't any p'liceman's suit.
Jim
I Have.
John What color's Jim's pajamas?
We White and blue.
Jim Just like Yohn's.
John What color's Jim's eyes?
We Brown.
Jim What color's Yohn's eyes?
We Brown.
Jim Yust like Dim's.
John But my hair's different. What color's my hair?
We Dark brown.
John What color's Jim's hair?
We Yellow.
Jim Oh, Yah.
John What color's people's faces?
We Different colors.
John What color's my face?
We Whitish and brownish and flesh color.
John What color's Jim's face?
We White and pink.
Jim Oh, yah.
John Is everybody's faces different colors?
We No. Yours is about the same color as lots of people's.
Jim Me, too.
John Is anybody's face red?
We Yes, some people's.
John Is my face red?
We Sometimes.
John Is Jim's?
We Sometimes.
John Are Indians?
We Yes.
Jim Oh, yah.
John Where do Indians live?
We Out west.
John I know which the two coldest are.
We What?
John West and south's the warmest and east and north's the coldest.
Jim Oh, yah.
John West is California and Yellowstones Park and east is New York and south's the South Pole and north's the North Pole.
Jim Who's the pole?
John It isn't the pole. It's the North Pole and South Pole.
Jim Oh, yah.
John Look! Jim's spilling his mile.
Jim I am not.
John It's spilled all over. He spilled it on the floor.
Jim It spilled itself.
John What's today?
We Friday.
John What comee after Friday?
We Saturday.
John What comes after Friday?
We Sunday.
John And does Doc-a-Yak come Sunday?
We Yes.
John Why don't Doc-a-Yak come every day.
We Once a week is a great plenty.
Jim Oh, yah.
