Wotdaell?

A Play in Two Scenes

Characters

  • C. W. Murf, a Wonder.

  • Vic Saier, a Complete Letter Writer.

  • Reporters, Office Attachés, etc.

Wotdaell?

Scene I

Mr. Murf's office, one day last week. Discovered are Mr. Murf, attachés, and the reporters.

Mr. Murf Well, whats new in the Federal league?
A Reporter The St. Louis Feds are after Vic Saier.
Mr. Murf Well, I've got a better first baseman than Vic Saier.
An Attaché That's right.
Another Attaché That's right.
All Reporters Good night, Nurse.

Scene II

A, or The, Hotel at Lansing. Time, last Sunday. Discovered, Mr. Murf. Enter Vic Saier.

Mr. Murf Well, Vic, I hear you have some offers from the Federal league.
Saier Yes, sir.
Mr. Murf For how much?
Saier So-and-so.
Mr. Murf Well, I'll give you So-and-so a year if you'll sign a three-year contract.
Saier All right.

Scene III

Mr. Murf's office, last Monday. Discovered, Mr. Murf and Attachés. Enter Reporters.

Mr. Murf Well, boys, I've received Saier's signed contract and a typewritten letter from Saier. He wrote the letter and mailed the contract Saturday. The letter says he will stick to the Cubs, "an organization which the public respects."
All Reporters Sounds just like Vic.
A Reporter Where were you Sunday?
Mr. Murf I went up to Lansing and signed Saier.
A Reporter Did you meet his stenographer?
Another Reporter Did Saier write the letter and mail the contract before or after you went to Lansing and signed him?
Mr. Murf I have nothing to say.
