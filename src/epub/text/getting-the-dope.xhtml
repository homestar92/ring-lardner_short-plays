Getting the Dope

Characters

  • John Jones, a Young Pitcher.

  • John Smith, a Young Pitcher.

  • H. Oswald Barlow, a Reporter.

  • T. Wilson Barrett, a Reporter.

Getting the Dope

Scene— Tampa, Fla.

Scene I

The hotel lobby. It is evening. Barrett and Barlow meet at the desk.

Barlow Sent any mail stuff yet?
Barrett No, but I've got to get some away tomorrow.
Barlow What are you going to hand them?
Barrett O, the boss wants brief sketches of all the youngsters.
Barlow So does mine. Let's split it up.
Barrett All right. Who shall we start on?
Barlow You mean, Whom shall we start on?
Barrett All right. I'll tell you—you get the dop on Smith and I'll take Jones.
Barlow All right. Which ones are they?
Barrett They're in the writing room. The boob with the soft shirt and the three days' growth is Smith. The light haired guy with the teeth sticking out is Jones.
Barlow Well, let's go to it.
Exeunt.

Scene II

A part of the Writing Room. Smith is seated at a desk gnawing a pen and trying to remove the ink from his fingers with a blotter. Enter Barlow.

Barlow Is this Mr. SMith?
Smith Yes. Whadd'ya want?
Barlow I'm Barlow of the Chicago Mail, Mr. Smith. I want to get some dope about you.
Smith Aw, they ain't no dope about me.
Barlow Let's see. You were with the Racine club last year?
Smith Uh-huh.
Barlow What was your record up there?
Smith Aw, I didn't pay no attention to no record.
Barlow How long have you been playing ball?
Smith Aw, I dunno.
Barlow You're a left-hander, aren't you?
Smith Sure. Wotinell did ya think I am?
Barlow How did you happen to start playing league ball?
Smith Aw, I dunno. I just started, that's all.
Barlow Well, much obliged to you, Mr. Smith
Smith Sure. That's all right.

Scene III

Another part of the Writing Room. Jones is seated at a desk gnawing a pen and trying to remove hte ink from his fingers with a blotter. Enter Barrett.

Barrett This is Mr. Jones, isn't it?
Jones Yeh.
Barrett I'm Barrett of the Chicago Globe. The paper wants some stuff about you.
Jones Aw, I don't want you to say nothin' about me in the paper.
Barrett O, we have to write something about all of you. You came here in the draft from Kewanee, didn't you?
Jones I guess so. I don't know nothin' about it.
Barrett You're right-handed, aren't you?
Jones Do you mean, Am I right-handed?
Barrett Yes.
Jones Sure, I am. Wotinell did ya think I was?
Barrett Was last year your first in league baseball?
Jones I dunno. Is that all you want?
Barrett Yes, that'll do. And I'm much obliged to you.
Jones Sure. Please to meetcha.

Scene IV

A Room in the Hotel. Barlow is seated at a typewriter. Enter Barrett.

Barrett What are you doing? Getting some of your mail stuff off?
Barlow Yes. Giving them the dope on Smith.
Barrett What kind of a boob is he?
Barlow A nut. Did you get anything on Jones?
Barrett Yes. He's a rum right. Get a pencil and I'll give you what I've got.
Barlow All right.
They compare notes.

Scene V

Another Room in the Hotel. Jones is seated on a bed, counting his money. Enter Smith.

Smith One o' them reporters just tried to pump me.
Jones One of 'em was after me a while ago. He didn't learn nothin'.
Smith I should say not. I wouldn't give none o' them boobs no infumation.
Jones He ast me my whole fam'ly hist'ry. I told him to beat it or I'd knock his block off.
Smith They got a lot o' nerve.

Scene VI

The Tampa Ball Park. A week has elapsed. Jones is sitting on the ground, resting. Smith approaches him.

Smith One o' the fellahs got some Chicago papers and I was just lookin' 'em over. When I see that reporter I'm goin' to take a punch at him.
Jones What's the matter?
Smith They's a piece in there sayin' I ain't got no speed, but I got a curve ball. And it says I only been in baseball a year and prob'ly'll need more experience. And it says I wouldn't win no prize at no beauty show.
Jones You oughta punch that guy's nose.
Smith I'm goin' to. But you ought to of saw what they says about you.
Jones What?
Smith They says you was light ofr a big league pitcher and that you acted like you had a sore arm and that you was with Kewanee last year.
Jones I wasn't no such thing. I was with the Canton club. Too light, eh? Sore arm, eh? I'll show them boobs. I'll take a wallop at that guy the first time I see him.
Smith I'm goin to punch the guy that wrote that stuff about me. You can bet your dough on that.
Jones Jiggers! Here they come now.
Barrett and Barlow stroll across the field toward Jones and Smith.
Barlow Hello there, Smitty.
Barrett Hello, Jonesey.
Jones And Smith Hello, fellahs.
Barlow Been working hard?
Smith O, I don't mind a little work.
Barrett Hank said your arm was sore, Jonesey. How does it feel?
Jones O, it was sore, but it feels pretty good now. How's tricks?
Barlow Well, we've got to go over and see Hank. So long, fellahs.
Jones And Smith So long, fellahs.
Barrett See you later.
Barrett and Barlow pass on.
Smith I'm goin' to take a pinch at that guy some day.
Jones I'm goin' to wallop that other guy good when I get a chance.
