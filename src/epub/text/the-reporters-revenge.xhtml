The Reporter's Revenge

Or, the Thirst For Knowledge

Scene I

A place in the loop. Afternoon. Shano and Julius, mix artists; Marty, a citizen, and the Reporter.

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Shano Who? Me?
Reporter Yes. You.
Shano I impatic'ly don't think nothin' of it. I'm with the Nationals.
Marty Ditto.
Reporter What does that mean?
Shano What does what mean?
Reporter "Ditto."
Shano That means he thinks the same as me. "Ditto" dessinates one distinction.
Reporter To Julius. What do YOU think of the Federal league?
Julius It's goin' to be a big thing if Shano don' think nothin' of it.

Scene II

A place in the loop. Same afternoon. Billy Jones, bellhop, and the Reporter.

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Billy All right, boss; all right.
Reporter Do you mean you think it's all right?
Billy Dat's all right boss. I'm for 'em.

Scene III

A place in the loop. Same afternoon. A Traffic Cop and the Reporter.

Reporter Well, it's pretty windy.
Cop Yes, but it looks as though it might warm up.
Reporter Pretty near time.
Cop Yes. They'll be playin' ball pretty soon now.
Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Cop Me? O, as far as I'm concerned, they might as well be three or four leagues as one. I can't ge tout to the games. If the people wants 'em, all right. I won't stop 'em.
Reporter Do you think the Feds will stick?
Cop Do I think they'll stick? Well, they'll stick as far as I'm concerned. I won't stop 'em. Toot, toot.

Scene IV

A Place in the Loop. Same Afternoon. Vendor Of Novelties and the Reporter.

Vendor and to take somethin' home to amuse the children? Amuses the grown folks, too. Looks mighty simple, but there's a lot to it. Makes the kids laugh.
Reporter No, I guess not.
Vendor Let you have this for a dime and the regular—
Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Vendor Me? O, I don't know nothin' about it. Don't you want one o' these to take home to your kids?
Reporter Why don't you go to ⸻'s place? You ought to sell some there, they call that the Rube's home.
Vendor I've often saw you there.
Reporter But you never seen me buy any of your stuff.

Scene V

A Place in the Loop. Same Afternoon. Johnnie, Mix Artist, and the Reporter.

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Johnnie Well, it's a good deal like a horse.
Reporter How's that?
Johnnie That's the way I look at it; it's a good deal like a horse.
Reporter How do you mean?
Johnnie Well, you can't never tell nothin' about a horse till you see what kind of a track he's runnin' on.
Reporter What's that got to do with it?
Johnnie Got a whole lot to do with it. The Feds is just like some horse. You can't tell nothin' about 'em till you see what kind of track he's runnin' on. They're just like a horse.
Reporter Well, that's five horses. You must be stuck by this time.

Scene VI

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. A Lady Cashier and the Reporter.

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Lady What do I know about it? You're tryin' to kid somebody.
Reporter No, it's on the square. I want it for the paper.
Lady Don't you put my name in the paper.
Reporter How do you spell it?
She spells it.
Reporter Now, what do you think of the Federal league?
Lady I don't know anything about it I'm sore at them for what they did to Johnnie Evvers. I'm a fan for whatever team he's on. Boston Red Sox, isn't it?

Scene VII

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. Kennedy and Fitch, Mix Artists and the Reporter

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Kennedy Just a big gamble. That's the way I look at it. Just a big gamble.
Reporter to Fitch. What do YOU think of the Federal league?
No answer.

Scene VIII

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. Happy, a waiter, and the Reporter

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Happy Well, I don't know what to think. If they got plenty o' money and is willin' to spend it I don't see no reason why they shouldn't ought to make it a go but still from all I can learn Ban Johnson and them is goin' to make a hot fight and they prob'ly got more money if they put it all together but still if the Fed'rals stick together and fight and give the public good baseball I don't see why they shouldn't ought to make good right off the reel if they play the right kind o' ball and play high class ball and get the crowds out but still you can't tell nothin' about it.

Scene IX

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. Ben, Mix Artist, and the Reporter.

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Ben Wot you ast me for? You know I don't know nuttin' about it. I hear 'em ravin' round here, but I can't get no sense to it. Baseball or no baseball, wot do I care. I'm no criterion.

Scene X

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. Joe, a Neway, and the Reporter

Reporter How's everything?
Joe Rotten, rotten.
Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Joe Do you want ot know what I think o' the Fed'ral league?
Reporter Yes.
Joe Well, I ain't thought much about it. If it's a go, it's a go, and if it ain't, it ain't. That's all they is to it. Anyway, they ain't neither one o' the three teams here wort adam. The Sox never was no good and the Cubs gave away all the men they had. McGraw and Coney Mack's got the only teams and if the Cubs was to get hold o' somethin' good, COney Mack'd take 'em away from it. Murphy made his big mistake when he let Chance go. I wouldn't go to none o' their games if you hired me. They're all a bunch o' burglars.

Scene XI

A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. Cliff, a Writer, and the Reporter

Reporter What do you think of the Federal league?
Cliff What is the Federal league?
Reporter I don't know.
Cliff I've got a better question that that for you.
Reporter Shoot.
Cliff What'll you have?
