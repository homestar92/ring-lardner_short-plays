Doc's Breakfast

Scene—Dining room in Dr. Brown's home. The table set for breakfast. On a small table near the wall is a telephone. Just before the curtain rises, the bell is heard ringing and Mrs. Brown is answering the phone.

Mrs. Brown Yes. I'll call him She goes to a door and calls Jake! Telephone.
After a moment's pause Dr. Brown enters. He is fully dressed in clothes that have evidently been slept in. He goes to the phone.
Dr. Brown This is Dr. Brown.
Voice At Other End O, Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Gordon. My baby has a terrible cough and his head's stopped up and I think he's got a fever.
Dr. Brown I'll drop in in a little while. He hangs up. Well, I guess I'll go back to bed.
Mrs. Brown Back to bed! Why it's a quarter to 5. You've slept forty minutes. Your breakfast's all ready.
Dr. Brown I had no idea it was so late. I'll— The phone rings and he answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
Voice At Other End This is Mrs. Gordon again. My baby's still sick.
Dr. Brown All right, Mrs. Gordon. I'll be over presently. He hangs up.
Mrs. Brown East your sausage before it gets cold.
Dr. Brown Um! Nice country sausage. The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Parker. Signe just woke up with a high fever and she's got a bad cough and feels terribly weak.
Dr. Brown All right. I'll be over in a few minutes. He hangs up. The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Russell again. Harold wouldn't drink the milk.
Dr. Brown Well, don't force it on him. I'll be over in a few minutes. He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table. This looks— The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. This is Mrs. Parker again. Signe's still sick.
Dr. Brown All right. I'll be up there pretty soon. He hangs up. He sits down at the breakfast table. This looks— The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. This is Mrs. Russell again. Harold just drank half the milk and he doesn't seem a bit better.
Dr. Brown Give him the rest of it. I'll be there in a few minutes. He hangs up and resumes his seat at the breakfast table. This looks like— The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. O, Dr. Brown. This is Joe Lewis. The wife went to bed sick last night and she's worse this morning. She's got a temperature and a cough and feels weak.
Dr. Brown I'll be over presently. He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table. This looks like the— The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. This is Mrs. Russell again. Harold finished his milk and now he's coughing.
Dr. Brown I'll be there presently. He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table. This looks like the same piece of sausage that was here yesterday. The phone rings. He answers it. This is Dr. Brown.
V. At O. E. This is Mrs. Parker. Signe doesn't seem to get any better and there'll be nobody to cook Pete's breakfast.
Dr. Brown I'll be over right away. He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table. This looks like the same piece of sausage that was here yesterday.
Mrs. Brown Of course, it does. It is. It's the same piece that was here Christmas week.
Dr. Brown Well, I'll try to get rid of it. He takes up a fork.
Mrs. Brown Looking at the clock Great heavens! Do you know it's 5 i'clock and after!
Dr. Brown jumping up from the table I'll have to run! He starts for the hall. Wrap up that piece of sausage.
Mrs. Brown Have you got everything? Got a city directory?
Dr. Brown Yes, everything.
Mrs. Brown Where can I find you if I want you?
Dr. Brown In the 600 block on Wayne street for the next half hour. The 700 block the half hour after that. And so on. And call the drug store and tell them to make up 14,000 No. 876A Prescriptions. 'Bye.
She hands him a kiss and the sausage wrapped up. He starts for the front door. The phone rings. Mrs. Brown answers it.
Mrs. Brown To Dr. Brown It's Mrs. Parker. She says Signe's still sick. She—
Dr. Brown
Tell her I'm bringing Pete's breakfast with me. (He goes out.
