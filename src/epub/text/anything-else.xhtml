Anything Else?

Characters

  • The Doorman.

  • A Man from the East.

Scene—The Reception Room.

Doorman Did you wish to see somebody?
Man I want to see the sporting editor.
Doorman He won't be down until this evening.
Man Well, maybe you can tell me what I want to know.
Doorman What is it?
Man Detroit had a pitcher named Clauss and they let him go.
Doorman Well?
Man Where is he at?
Doorman I can't tell you. You'd have to see somebody in the sporting department.
Man I think he went to Jackson in the Southern Michigan League.
Doorman I don't know, I'm sure.
Man I'm from the east.
Doorman Is that so?
Man If I should go into an eastern newspaper office they'd tell me in a minute.
Doorman Tell you what?
Man What team Clauss is with. He's a friend of mine.
Doorman Well, I'm sorry, but I can't tell you.
Man Can a man go from here to Kalamazoo by trolley?
Doorman I don't know. I guess you could find out by looking in the railway guide.
Man Well, I guess I'll look in a railway guide.
Doorman Yes.
Man Suppose I find out I can't get there by trolley?
Doorman Well?
Man I'd have to stay here all night.
Doorman You could take a train.
Man No, I'd have to stay here.
Doorman Well?
Man Where's the headquarters of the K. P. in Chicago?
Doorman I don't know. You could probably find out by looking in a directory.
Man So you can't tell me where Clauss is at?
Doorman No.
Man Well, much obliged to you.
