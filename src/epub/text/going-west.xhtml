Going West

Scene—The Varsity car on the Michigan Special. The players are scattered about the car and the coach walks up and down the aisle.

The Coach Where was you on No. ⸻?
Maulbetsch Where was I? Why, coach, the minute the ball was passed, zowie! three men hit me all at once. I never was hit so hard in my life.
The Coach Did you know what the signal was?
Maulbetsch Why, sure, but what could I do?
The Coach Why didn't you tell Hughitt to give you the ball?
Maulbetsch I'm not running the team
The Coach When you boys get in there you forget everything that's ever been taught you.
Maulbetsch The luck was against us, Coach.
The Coach The luck was no such a thing. You could have had two touchdowns just like that. It wasn't the luck's fault and it wasn't Harvard's fault. It wasn't anybody's fault but your own fault.
Maulbetsch I gave you the best I had, Coach.
The Coach I know you did, Mauly. But it's a dag-gone shame to have a game right in your hands and then throw it away. What are you doing there, Splawn?
Splawn Just looking at the paper.
The Coach You'd better get out the signals and look at 'em. That's what I want you to do all week, study those signals. If you could carry the signals round in your pocket during the game, you'd be all right. But instead of that you get out on the field and the signals are in the dressing room and you can't look at 'em. The place for the signals to be is right in your head and instead of that you leave 'em on a piece of paper in the dressing room.
Splawn Uh-huh.
The Coach What were you trying to do, make up a play?
Splawn I couldn't pass, Coach. They were right on top of me.
The Coach Of course, they were right on top of you, but if you'd been where you should have been there wouldn't have been anybody near you. The Coach pulls out pencil and paper. You should have been right cheer. Instead of that you were right cheer. No wonder they were on top of you. What was you thinking about, Tommy?
Hughitt I guess I pulled a boner, Coach.
The Coach You must have been afraid we'd run up too big a score.
Hughitt I pulled a boner.
The Coach What have I been telling you all week? If you got inside their ten yard line you were to use No. ⸻ and No. ⸻.
Hughitt It was my fault, Coach.
The Coach It wasn't all your fault. WHy didn't some of those linemen tell you when they knew you were doing the wrong thing? Were all you fellow dumb? And when Raynsford saw Splawn wasn't in the right place, what did he pass the ball for? Why didn't some of those linemen holler "Signals"? Were you fellows all dumb? Why didn't you say something, McHale?
Mchale I had all I could do to handle my own work.
The Coach Well, if you'd all handled your own work right they couldn't have licked us, not even with our bonehead playing.
An Outsider We'll get 'em next year.
Coach Yost Yes, next year. Maybe we'll get 'em next year and maybe we won't. But we had 'em this year and all we had to do was use a little common sense. It didn't need any brains. All it needed was for you boys to keep your eyes open and remember your signals. THe whole football game could have been won just like that, and that.
Another Outside We outplayed 'em at that.
The Coach It doesn't get us anything to outplay 'em. They got seven points and we didn't get any. That's all there is to it. Those seven points are a whole lot better than outplaying 'em. But if you fellows hadn't been unconscious we'd have had fourteen points and they wouldn't have had any. Then you wouldn't have had to be talking about outplaying 'em.
Maulbetsch I gave you all I had, Coach.
The Coach I know you did, Mauly.
Maulbetsch I did all I could.
The Coach Yes, but what was you waiting for on No. ⸻?
Maulbetsch I was waiting ofr that end to come.
The Coach Yes, and you waited till they all came up and shook hands with you. The trouble with you boys is that you forget all you've been taught the minute you get out there.
Maulbetsch I gave you all I had, Coach.
The Coach I know you did, Mauly. But what you boys need is to have your heads examined. A little common sense meant the football game. We could have won it just like that, and like that. Huh?
