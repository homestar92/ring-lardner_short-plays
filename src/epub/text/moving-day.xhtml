Moving Day

Characters Represented.

  • The Mister, a Husband and Father.

  • The Missus, a Wife and Mother.

  • The Baby, a Baby.

Scene One and Only

Mister Where shall we put these?
Missus On top of the other books and then a rug on top of them.
Baby My book.
Missus No, no. Don't touch those.
Mister Get away from here.
Baby Ah, sit here.
Mister No, no. You can't sit there. Go out on the front porch and play with Duke.
Baby Play with Duke.
Missus Where shall we put Duke?
Mister On top of this barrel.
Baby Duke top of barrel.
Mister Get away. Daddy said get away. You must play with baby doll.
Baby Play with Duke on porch.
Missus No, that's all off. Play with Kitty.
Baby Me play with Kitty.
Mister Where shall we put Kitty?
Missus Is there room in the trunk?
Baby Room in tunk.
Mister Say good-by to Kitty, babe.
Baby Goo-by, Kitty. W'ere is Kitty?
Missus You'll see Kitty in a few days.
Baby See Kitty.
Mister In a few days.
Baby Foo days.
Missus You can't sit there.
Baby Can't sit dere.
Missus Why don't you go on the porch and play with baby doll?
Baby No‑o‑o‑o‑o. Sit dere.
Mister Go away.
Baby No‑o‑o‑o.
Missus You're in daddy's way.
Baby Daddy's way.
Mister Yes. Get up. Go out on the front porch.
Baby Play f'ont porch baby doll.
Missus No. Baby doll's all gone.
Baby Baby doll a' gone. W'ere Baby doll?
Mister You mustn't sit there. Go out in the kitchen and play with horsey.
Baby Sit here. Read book.
Missus Books all gone.
Baby W'ere books?
Mister Why don't you go and find your ball?
Baby We're ball? Ball a' gone. W'ere ball?
Missus You mustn't sit there.
Mister No, no, baby. Get away.
Baby Get away.
Mister Here, here. You can't stand there.
Baby Stand dere.
Missus No, you mustn't stand there. Go away, baby.
Baby Go 'way, baby.
Mister Here. Don't stand on that. You might break it. Bad boy!
Baby Bad boy. Stand on that.
Missus You mustn't.
Baby Mustn't stand that.
Mister Daddy'll have to shut you up.
Baby Daddy, shut up.
Missus O, look! You've broken that dish. Bad boy!
Baby Bad boy. Boke dish.
Mister You ought to be ashamed of yourself.
Baby Shame se'f.
Missus Why don't you go in the kitchen and play with spoons?
Baby Play spooons.
Mister Here, here. Get away. Daddy's working. Go away. Got out on the porch and see horsey.
Baby See horsey. Play horsey.
Missus Don't stand on that book.
Baby Stand book.
Mister Mother's packing that book.
Baby Read a book.
Missus You can read the book after a while.
Baby Read a book.
Mister After awhile.
Baby Read a book.
Missus Please, please, baby. Don't stand there.
Baby Read a book. Stand dere.
Mister Read a book and stand there after awhile.
Baby Read a book.
Missus Tomorrow you can read a book.
Baby Read a book. Stand dere. After 'while. 'Morrow.
Mister O, get away from here.
Baby Read book. Get 'way from here. Stand dere. Read book. After 'while. 'Morrow.
Missus O, please, baby. Mother just fixed that.
Baby Fix dat. Fix dat. Read a book. 'Morrow. Fix dat.
Mister No, no, no. You mustn't do that.
Baby Do date. Read a book. This here. Dere. 'Morrow. Baby doll. Kitty. See dat.
Missus O, I do wish you would go out on the porch and look at horseys.
Baby No, no. No‑o‑o‑o‑o. Stay and read a book. Dere.
Mister Listen, baby. If you'll go out on the porch and look at the horseys, Daddy'll read all about "All Aboard, Conductor Man" in a little while.
Baby All gourd. 'Ductor man, now.
Missus No, no. You mustn't sit there.
Baby Aside. Wyinell should we move anyway. I always had a good time here.
