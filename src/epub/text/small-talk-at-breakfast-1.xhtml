Small Talk at Breakfast (I)

Cast of Characters

  • John

  • Jim

  • Their Dad

Jim Is that your paper?
Their Dad Yes.
Jim Who paper is it?
T. D. My paper.
John Who makes the paper?
T. D. Lots of people.
John Does God make the paper?
T. D. I don't think so.
Jim Is God outdoors?
T. D. Yes.
John Are giants outdoors?
T. D. Yes.
John How old are giants?
T. D. A thousand years.
John How old are they when they're born?
T. D. A hundred years.
John And when they're as old as I am, are they a thousand years? Are they big?
T. D. Yes.
Jim Oh, yah.
John I can count to a thousand from one.
T. D. I believe you.
Jim I can count, too.
John Listen! Jim says he can count. He can't count.
Jim I CAN!
John
You can't; you're too little.
Jim
I Am Not.
T. D. Never mind. You can both count.
Jim I'm big.
T. D. Sure you are.
John How do pigs eat?
T. D. gives an imitation of pigs at their victuals.
John Is that the way?
T. D. Yes.
Jim Oh, yah. And how does horseys eat?
Another imitation.
Jim Oh, yah. Dat's the way.
John How do donkeys eat?
T. D. Just like horses.
Jim How does monkeys eat?
T. D. Just like donkeys.
John THEY DO NOT!
T. D. No, no. You're right. This is how.
Imitation of a monkey eating.
John Will you read me after breakfast?
T. D. I have to work.
Jim Where is your work?
T. D. In the other room?
Jim Go and work.
T. D. I will when I get ready.
Jim Work now.
T. D.. I'm not ready.
John Do all big men work?
T. D. No.
John Why do you work?
T. D. So you can eat.
Jim How does mices eat?
Another imitation.
Jim Oh, yah. Do mices' daddies work?
T. D. Yes.
John Do big men have chicken pox?
T. D. No.
John Will you put it in the paper that I've got chicken pox?
T. D. I have put it in.
John Do Lester and Sterling know I've got chicken pox?
T. D. Yes, if they read the paper.
John They can't read.
Jim I can read.
John Listen! Jim says he can read.
Jim I CAN read.
John You can't!
Jim I CAN!
John You're too little.
Jim I AM NOT!
Exit T. D.
