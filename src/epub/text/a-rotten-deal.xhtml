A Rotten Deal

Characters.

  • James Callahan, a Manager.

  • Bill Lamewing, a Recruit.

  • Joe Patience, his Roommate.

Scene— Paso Robles and other parts of heaven. Time—Any spring.

Act I

The first day in camp.

Scene I

Lamewing's room in the hotel. It is late afternoon. Joe is addressing postcards with a pencil. Enter Bill.

Bill Whadd'ya think o' that guy, tryin' to work us to death the first day? He didn't make me kill myself. I ain't no boob and I ain't takin' no chances o' throwin' my arm away. I'll take my time and when I feel like cuttin' loose, I'll cut loose. And then I'll show 'em somethin'. Is this your comb? Let me use it oncet, will ya?
Joe I guess that's right.
Bill You know it's right. Don't let no manager run ya. First thing you know, your whip'll be gone and they'll be sendin' ya back to the sticks.
Joe Sure.
Bill If Callahan asks me why I didn't cut loose today, I'll give him a talkin' to that'll hold him awhile.
Joe Sure.

Scene II

The dining room. Callahan and Bill are seated at a table.

Callahan Well, I didn't notice that you hurt yourself today.
Bill I was afraid o' workin' my arm too much.
Callahan I don't want you to overwork your arm. I meant that you didn't show any life out there.
Bill I wasn't feelin' good.
Callahan Well, get a good rest tonight and then get to work tomorrow.
Bill All right.

Act II

The second day.

Scene I

The practice field. The players are engaged in batting practice.

Callahan Get out and pitch a few to the batters, Lamewing. You don't need to put anything on it.
Bill My arm's pretty sore, Jim.
Callahan All right, then. Wait till tomorrow.

Scene II

A part of the hotel lobby. Bill and Joe are seated in adjoining chairs.

Bill That guy tried to make me kill myself today. He told me to go out and throw to the batters and put a lot o' stuff on the ball. You ought to have heard me bawl him. I bawled him good.
Joe Yeh, I heard ya.

Act III

The sixth day.

Scene I

The practice field. The players are engaged in a practice game.

Callahan Warm up, Lamewing. I'm going to work you the last two innings.
Bill I don't feel good, Jim.
Callahan What's the matter? Your arm still sore?
Bill No, I don't feel good.

Scene II

A part of the hotel lobby. Bill and Joe are seated in adjoining chairs.

Bill Callahan tried to get me to go out there and pitch part o' that game today. And me with a rotten cold. He ought to of knew better. You ought to of heard me come back at him. I give it to him good.
Joe I'll bet you did.

Act IV

The tenth day.

Scene I

The ball park in San Francisco. It is just before game time.

Callahan Warm up, Lamewing. I'm going to start you. Take it easy.
Lamewing I don't feel good, Jim.
Callahan I didn't think you would You've been stuffing yourself in that dining room.
Lamewing No, no, Jim. I feel good all over except my arm. I must o' strained it.
Callahan What doing? Shoveling potatoes?

Scene II

A part of the Oakland hotel lobby. Bill and Joe are seated in adjoining chairs. It is evening.

Bill You made a sucker o' yourself today. You went five innin's like as if it was mid-summer. I wouldn't kill myself for nobody.
Joe I didn't hurt myself. I feel good.
Bill Yes, you feel good now. But wait till along about April. Your arm'll be gone and they'll slip you to the sticks.

Act V

The twentieth day.

Scene I

The ball park at Los Angeles. It is just before game time.

Callahan Scott, you and Benz work this game. You start, Scotty. How do you feel today, Lamewing?
Lamewing Me? I feel great.
Callahan Wait a minute, Scotty. I guess I'll start Lamewing. Warm up, Lamewing.
Lamewing I don't feel good, Jim.

Scene II

A part of the Los Angeles hotel lobby. It is evening. Lamewing is seated in an easy chair. Enter Callahan.

Callahan Lamewing, I've sold you to Tacoma. You didn't show me anything and I can't afford to carry you along. You can get a train out of here tonight. Here's your transportation. Good by. No hard feelings, I hope.
Bill No, no. That's all right, Jim. Good by, Jim.
Exit Callahan.

Scene III

Another part of the lobby. Joe is standing within reaching distance of a cuspidor. Enter Bill.

Bill Just as I thought. That slob has canned me. I'm sold to Tacoma. And he didn't have the nerve to tell me so. He wrote me a note and then blowed out o' the hotel. I wish he'd told me to my face like a square guy. I'd of took a wallop at him.
Joe Sure.
Bill I got a rotten deal from this club. They didn't give me no chance.
Joe That's right. You wasn't worked at all, was ya?
Bill No, sir. I didn't pitch an innin'; never had a chance. Well, I'm goin' up to the room and pack up. I'll see ya when I come down. I hope Callahan gets back here before train time. I want to bust him one.
Exit.

Scene IV

A room in the hotel. It is late at night. Enter Joe.

Joe Well, he's gone. Goes over to the dresser. I wonder wotinell I done with my comb. Goes into the bathroom. And my toothbrush, too. Wotinell kind o' hotel is this anyway?
