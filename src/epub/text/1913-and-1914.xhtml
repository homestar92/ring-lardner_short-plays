1913 and 1914

1913

Characters.

  • Butch Swatem, a Ath-a-lete.

  • A. Magnate, Owner of the Club.

Scene—A. Magnate's Office.

A. Magnate is seated at his desk. Butch enters from the reception room.

Butch How are you, Mr. Magnate?
Magnate opens and reads thirty letters, smokes a few cigars, and then picks up a magazine.
Butch How are you, Mr. Magnate?
Magnate I'm busy.
Butch I won't take more than a minute of your time. I want to see about signin' up.
Magnate O, see my secretary. I haven't no time to monkey with your ball players.
Butch I already seen your secretary and he said I'd have to see you.
Magnate reads that month's installment of a serial story.
Magnate You still here?
Butch Yeh. I want to see about signing' up.
Magnate Well, you've got your contract, haven't ya?
Butch Yeh.
Magnate Well, w'y don't ya sign and be done with it?
Butch I ain't satisfied with it.
Magnate W'at is it you ain't satisfied with?
Butch The salary.
Magnate Well, say, if you don't like that salary go take a jump in the lake. I havn't no time to talk to ya.
Butch I won't never play no ball for this money.
Magnate I should worry. If you won't play ball I'll get somebody else.
Butch You can't get nobody like me.
Magnate I don't want nobody like you.
Butch I'm through with you guys.
Magnate Beat it. I've got a lot o' work to do.
Butch Ain't you goin' to do nothin' about this hear contract?
Magnate Will I have to call a officer?
Butch Yes, call a officer.
Magnate Well, I will call a officer.
Butch Yeh, go ahead and call a offier.
Magnate Well, I will.
Butch Ain't you goin' to give me w'at I want?
Magnate Listen now. I'm through with ya. If you don't like it you can do the other thing. See? Now, beat it!

1913

Characters.

  • Butch Swatem, a Ath-a-lete.

  • A. Magnate, Owner of the Club.

Scene—A. Magnate's Office.

A. Magnate is seated at his desk. Butch enters the reception room. A. Magnate jumps out of his chair.

Magnate How are ya, Mr. Swatem? How are ya, Butch? You don't mind if I call you Butch, do ya?
Butch Mr. Swatem to me, please.
Magnate I beg your pardon, Mr. Swatem. What can I do for ya, old boy?
Butch I come about this here contract you sent me. I won't sign it, see?
Magnate Well, well, I don't blame ya. It was a clerical error. I won't sign it, see?
Butch Why don't you git a secretary that knows somethin'?
Magnate I'll discharge him tomorrow.
Butch How about this here contract?
Magnate Well, my dear mr. Swatem, fill it in to suit yourself.
Butch All right.
He fills it in to suit himself.
Magnate There, that's all right. I hope that's satisfactory to you.
Butch This here is all right now. The contract's all right. I ain't got no kick comin' on that.
Magnate Well, what else can I do for ya?
Butch You're chewin' gum, ain't ya? Cut it out.
Magnate removes the gum from his mouth.
Magnate There. I beg your pardon. I didn't know you objected to it.
Butch Where did ya get that there fountain pen?
Magnate O, that? That was a present to me.
Butch Say, I want that. See? I want it.
Magnate Sure. All right. Take it.
Butch Thanks. W'at are ya goin' to do tonight?
Magnate Me? I'm going home to supper.
Butch That's fine. I'll go with ya.
Magnate Well, that's great. We'll be glad to have ya.
Butch What are ya goin' to have?
Magnate I think it's pork chops.
Butch I don't like 'em. See? Get somethin' else.
Magnate All right, all right. I'll call up the missus.
Butch Well, so long. I'll come round about five bells.
Magnate Wait a minute, Mr. Swatem. You got some dust on your shoes.
He produces a silk handkerchief.
