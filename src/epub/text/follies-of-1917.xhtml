Follies of 1917

Scene I

Music Room of Pat Moran's home

Song—Pat Moran

There's a Baker down
In Quakertown
Who needed dough, 'twould seem
He has it now,
But Holy Cow!
I ain't got no team!

Scene II

Third base at Comiskey Park. An October afternoon. Scenery by Urban Faber.

Herr Zimmerman "Mr. Weaver, there's some one calling on you."
Mr. Weaver "Who is it?"
Herr Zimmerman "His name is Faber."
Mr. Weaver "Tell him I'm not home yet."

Scene III

Music room in the home of Cornelius McGillicuddy.

Song—Connie Mack

I do not know who'll land on top;
I do not care a rip.
I only know I'm sure to cop
The seller championship.

Scene IV

Infield at the Polo Grounds.

Herr Zimmerman having popped the side out again, walks toward his position "You ain't showed me nothin', Sy-cot."

Scene V

Comiskey Park. A Saturday afternoon in October.

Song—Chorus of White Sox

Of all the men New York has got
There's none as sweet as Sallee.
The stuff he's got's the stuff we swat,
For he pitches in our alley.

Scene VI

Bedroom in a Texas hotel.

Herr Herzog "Kamerad!"

Scene VII

Office at the American league park, Philadelphia.

Mr. Shibe "I had an awful nightmare last night. I dreamed we had Ty Cobb."
Stenographer "Nightmare!"
Mr. Shibe "Yes. The understanding was we had to keep him."

Scene VIII

Polo Grounds. A Monday afternoon in October.

Collins at third base "I can beat you running, Heine."
Zim "Yes, you can!"
Collins "I'll say I can"
They start.
Collins looking round "I'm a faster man than you are, Heine Zim."
This line is copped from Friend Tad.
Zim "Yes, you are!"
Collins "I'll say so!"
Rariden "Throw the ball."
Zim "Who'll I throw it to? Klem?"
Holke at first base "This is a mighty exciting game. I'm glad I could get to see it."

Scene IX

A street in Pittsburgh

First Citizen "I see that Barney's arranging for the club to play exhibition games at the army camps."
Second Citizen "Well, by spring the soldiers'll be tough enough to stand it."
